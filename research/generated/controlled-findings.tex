Averaging equally over the other crossed factors, increasing degree from 4 to 128 changes the BSR8-to-direct ratio from 0.959 to 0.528 at RHS 8, and from 0.925 to 0.578 at RHS 64. Changing block size from 8 to 32 increases that ratio by factors of 2.014 and 3.099, respectively. The row-count contrast is much smaller at RHS 8 and unfavorable at RHS 64. Thus both degree and shape affect the performance boundary, and more block rows alone do not guarantee an advantage.
